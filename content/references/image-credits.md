## Part 1

**Candidate imperial examination drunk in cell** — Unknown artist, 1605. Historical print; source identified by Wikimedia Commons as a Princeton University Elman PDF. Public domain. Use with a caption identifying it as an illustration of examination cells rather than a universal record of candidate experience.
