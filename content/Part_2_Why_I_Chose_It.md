# Part 2 — Why I Chose It

I chose this topic because I believe education is important in both historical and modern societies. Education can provide people with opportunities to improve their lives and build a better future.

Initially, I assumed that examinations were a fair way to measure people's abilities because everyone had to follow the same rules and demonstrate their knowledge. However, I began to question whether examinations could truly provide equal opportunities when people came from different social and economic backgrounds.

This made me curious about the imperial examination system in China. Although examinations offered people a chance to become government officials, I wondered whether people from poorer families had the same opportunities as those from wealthy families who could afford better education and preparation.

I chose this question because I want to understand whether the imperial examination system genuinely promoted social mobility or whether it also helped maintain social inequality.
